About Bare Minimum

Our Mission

Help teenagers understand why they may feel tired or have low energy. Provide simple and realistic tips that can fit into everyday life.

Encourage healthy habits such as getting enough sleep, eating regularly, staying active, and managing stress. Make information easy to understand and not overwhelming.

Our Vision

Create a place where teenagers can learn about their energy and wellbeing without feeling judged. Encourage teenagers to make small changes rather than trying to change everything at once.

Help young people feel more confident about looking after themselves. Show that even small improvements can make a difference.

Our Values

Integrity: We provide honest and trustworthy information. Compassion: We understand that everyone has different experiences and challenges.

Empowerment: We encourage teenagers to make choices that can support their wellbeing. Simplicity: We keep our advice clear, realistic, and easy to follow.
